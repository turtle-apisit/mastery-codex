# ROC Curve and AUC

## Summary

**Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC)** depicts the relative trade-off
between **true positives (benefits)** and **false positives (costs)**. It is a
performance measurement for classification problems **at various threshold
settings**.

Reading the ROC space:

- The **best possible** prediction method yields a point in the **upper-left
  corner, coordinate (0,1)** — 100% sensitivity (no false negatives) and 100%
  specificity (no false positives).
- **Random guessing** gives a point along the **diagonal** — the "line of
  no-discrimination" from bottom-left to top-right, regardless of the positive
  and negative base rates. Flipping coins is the example.
- The curve is generated by **scanning a parameter**, such as a sensitivity or
  threshold setting. **Scanning a range is the benefit over a single metric like
  accuracy** — it covers a range of classification thresholds, not just one.

**Area Under the Curve (AUC)** exists because the ROC curve is good for looking
at a **single** classifier but is **less suited to comparing** classifiers — with
two crossing curves, which is better? AUC collapses the curve to one number so
classifiers can be compared.

```
AUC = 0.5  -> random guessing
AUC = 1.0  -> perfect classifier
```

**The rating bands:**

| AUC | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 0.9 – 1.0 | Excellent |
| 0.8 – 0.9 | Good |
| 0.7 – 0.8 | Fair |
| 0.6 – 0.7 | Poor |
| 0.5 – 0.6 | Failed |

## Key points

- AUC measures a classifier's **ability to distinguish between classes** and
  summarises the ROC curve. Higher AUC = better at separating positive from
  negative.
- Related course figures: the **performance metrics graph** (where specificity
  and accuracy sat near 1.0 for all thresholds, and a threshold of 0.992 would
  have given a perfect result) and the **precision-recall graph** (choose the
  parameter whose precision-recall pair is **closest to (1.0, 1.0)**, although
  what "best" means depends on the application).

## Watch out for

- The point of ROC is that **the classification threshold is a choice**. A single
  accuracy number silently fixes it at one value and hides the trade-off.
