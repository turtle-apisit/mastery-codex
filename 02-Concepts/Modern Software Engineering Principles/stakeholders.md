# Stakeholders

## Summary

The answer to **"whose needs?"** — customer, developer, or someone else?

**Stakeholders** are *all groups that are or might be affected by your
organization's actions and success* (NIST, "Criteria for Performance Excellence
2013–2014"):

- Customers, Users
- The workforce, Partners
- Governing boards, Stockholders
- Policy makers
- Local and professional communities

**Stakeholders specific to software development:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Customers | System Verifiers |
| End-users | Project Management |
| Product Management | R&D Management |
| System Engineering | Sales |
| Architects | Marketing |
| Software Developers | Services |

## Key points

- The customer who pays and the end-user who operates the system are **different
  stakeholders with different needs**, and they are frequently not the same
  person.
- Missing a stakeholder during requirements is a preventable process failure —
  "a new stakeholder appears with a veto" is one of the listed causes of the
  moving target problem.

## Watch out for

- In AI-enabled systems there is **one addition**: the **person the system decides
  about** is now a stakeholder, and **they never asked to be**. The patient the
  no-show model flags did not sign up to be modelled.
