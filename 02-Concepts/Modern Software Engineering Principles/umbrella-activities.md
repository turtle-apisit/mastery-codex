# Umbrella Activities

## Summary

Activities that run **across the entire process** rather than sitting at one
point in it:

- Software project management
- Risk management
- Formal technical reviews
- Software quality assurance
- Software configuration management
- Work product preparation and production
- Reusability management
- Documentation

## Key points

- The name is the idea: they cover everything underneath, continuously, rather
  than occurring once in sequence like Communication → Planning → Modeling.
- **Documentation** being an umbrella activity is the direct argument against a
  "documentation phase" — you cannot perform a phase without the documentation
  of the previous phase, so documentation cannot wait until the end.
- The same reasoning applies to **formal technical reviews** and **quality
  assurance**: testing only at the end is far too late.

## Watch out for

- Confusing these with the five process activities is the standard exam error.
  Process activities are **sequential and produce the product**; umbrella
  activities are **continuous and protect the process**.
