# Output and Outcome

## Summary

Two words that sound interchangeable and are not.

- **Outputs** — the **products, services or facilities that result from
  activities**. The thing you built and shipped.
- **Outcomes** — the **changes, benefits, learning or other effects that happen
  as a result** of those products, services, or activities. What changed in the
  world because you shipped it.

## Key points

- You control outputs directly. You only influence outcomes.
- A project can deliver every output on the plan and produce no outcome at all —
  the system was built, delivered, and nobody's situation improved.
- This is the same distinction that shows up later as **wants vs needs**: a
  requested feature is an output, the business reason behind it is an outcome.

## Watch out for

- Project reporting almost always measures outputs, because they are countable.
  The question worth asking is whether the outcome was ever defined.
