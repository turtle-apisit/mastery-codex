# Goals of Software Development

## Summary

The framing question of the whole course: what is software development actually
trying to achieve?

**The Project Management Triangle** — the four competing constraints every
project balances:

```
            SCOPE
              |
       QUALITY (centre)
         /        \
      COST ---- TIME
```

Scope, Cost, and Time are the three corners; **Quality** sits in the middle,
because it is what gets sacrificed when the other three are squeezed.

The end goal is **customer satisfaction** — satisfying **needs and expectations**.

## Key points

- You cannot fix all three corners independently. Adding scope without adding
  time or cost comes out of quality.
- Everything else in the course follows from this: the software is complex,
  intangible, and made by people who make mistakes; it exists to satisfy needs
  that change; and it is built by teams with **less time and money than they
  need**. Every technique in the syllabus is a response to one of those facts.

## Watch out for

- "Quality" is not a fourth thing you can trade away deliberately — it is the
  variable that moves silently when the other three are over-constrained.

## Prerequisites

None — this is a root concept for the subject.
