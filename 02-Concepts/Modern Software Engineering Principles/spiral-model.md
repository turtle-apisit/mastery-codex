# Spiral Model

## Summary

**Simplified form:** the **rapid prototyping model plus risk analysis preceding
each phase**.

**Full spiral model:**

- **Precede** each phase by:
  - **Alternatives**
  - **Risk analysis**
- **Follow** each phase by:
  - **Evaluation and planning of the next phase**

**The two dimensions of the spiral diagram:**

- **Radial dimension** — **cumulative cost to date**
- **Angular dimension** — **progress through the spiral**

**Strengths:**

- Suitable for **large-scale and complex** software
- **Risk handling** — it is easy to judge how much to test

**Weaknesses:**

- **Expensive and complex**

## Key points

- Risk is the organising idea. Every other model in the course orders work by
  phase or by importance; the spiral orders it by **risk**.
- The two dimensions are a favourite exam question: **radial = cost**,
  **angular = progress**.

## Watch out for

**Comparison of life-cycle models.** Each model has its own strengths and
weaknesses, and the criteria for choosing include: **the organization**, **its
management**, **the skills of the employees**, and **the nature of the product**.

The course's best suggestion is not to pick one:

> **"Mix-and-match" life-cycle model.**
